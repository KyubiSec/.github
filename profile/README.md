## 👋 Who We Are

**KyubiSec** is a Tunisian cybersecurity company. We design, build, and run cybersecurity competitions: CTFs, live pentest competitions, and hackathons. We also create the challenges, machines, and platforms that power them.

| 🏆 Events organized | 👥 Participants | 🚩 CTFs & pentest machines | 🥇 Competition winners |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **5** | **850+** | **350+** | **40+** |

---

## 🛠️ What We Do

- 🏗️ **Event Infrastructure**: reliable, secure infrastructure for competitions and events
- 🖥️ **Custom Event Platforms**: real-time scoring and participant management
- 📅 **Event Organization**: end to end, from planning to post-event analysis
- 🚩 **CTF & Pentest Machines**: custom challenges and vulnerable machines
- 🔍 **Security Assessment**: security reviews of event infrastructure and platforms
- 🤝 **Community Building**: workshops, webinars, and events for the community
