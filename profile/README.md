## Who We Are

KyubiSec is a Tunisian cybersecurity company. We design, build, and operate the competitions and the infrastructure behind them — CTFs, live pentest competitions, and hackathons — and we build the challenges, vulnerable machines, and platforms that power them.

We are a hands-on team. Between us we cover offensive security, hardware and IoT, blue-team operations, and the platform engineering that ties it together, and we take work end to end rather than hand it off.

## What We Do

- **Offensive security & penetration testing** — web, network, and application assessments carried through from recon to exploitation and reporting.
- **Hardware, IoT & physical security** — firmware and embedded analysis, IoT device testing, and physical-access assessments.
- **Blue team & defensive operations** — SOC build-out and automation, threat intelligence, and digital forensics / incident response.
- **Security platforms & tooling** — custom platforms for scoring, orchestration, and AI-driven security automation, built in-house.
- **Event infrastructure & organization** — reliable, isolated infrastructure and full event delivery, from planning through post-event analysis. We have run five events to date, including Tunisia's first large-scale live pentesting competition.
- **CTF & pentest machines** — original challenges and vulnerable machines across web, forensics, OSINT, reverse engineering, pwn, crypto, and stego.
